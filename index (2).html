How it works

From complaint to closure, in four recorded steps.
01

File

Describe the issue and its location. You get a ticket number immediately.

02

Acknowledge

The relevant ward office confirms receipt, usually within 48 hours.

03

Act

The issue is assigned and worked on. Status updates post to your ticket.

04

Resolve

Once fixed, the ticket closes with a note — and stays visible on record.
